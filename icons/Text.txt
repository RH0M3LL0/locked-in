--- Loading gear (loading-gear.svg) ---
The SVG is static on purpose: spinning the <img> with CSS runs on the GPU, which is
much smoother on slow Android phones and iPhones than animating inside the SVG.
<style>@keyframes sp{to{transform:rotate(360deg)}}
.gear{animation:sp 2.4s linear infinite;will-change:transform}
