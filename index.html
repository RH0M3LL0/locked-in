About the Computer and Robotics Education Portal

The portal of the Department of Computer and Robotics Education, Faculty of Vocational and Technical Education, University of Nigeria, Nsukka.

  • Results and CGPA: see your scores, grades and cumulative GPA for every session.
  • Course materials: download lecture PDFs and read AI-written summaries.
  • Tests and practice: take timed tests set by your lecturers and practise with AI drills.
  • Calendar and reminders: exam dates, deadlines and alerts before they happen.
  • Support: ask the AI assistant first, then send a ticket to the admin if you still need help.

Students sign in with their registration number. Lecturers and admins sign in with their email.

JavaScript is needed to sign in to the portal.
